# Paradise public-data package

`geography_2025.geojson` contains the Paradise town boundary (Census GEOID
`0655520`) and 1,038 clipped Butte County road geometries. It is generated by
`scripts/build_paradise_geography.py` from these US Census Bureau 2025
TIGER/Line files:

- [California places](https://www2.census.gov/geo/tiger/TIGER2025/PLACE/tl_2025_06_place.zip), SHA-256 `2b59dc5d54c69c7a451795401fc2a1c1c68b172f1d912d3486080e04a83e23e8`
- [Butte County roads](https://www2.census.gov/geo/tiger/TIGER2025/ROADS/tl_2025_06007_roads.zip), SHA-256 `b04bc57bd70365206a243d14e4197a62c775d4f56c7bd3aa82bcca9d1b4eb63c`

The 2025 roads are a geographic reference, **not** a verified emergency road
network: they contain no current closures, lane capacities, traffic speeds or
evacuation permissions. The API serves this static package without contacting
Census at runtime.

`camp_fire_final_2018_town_clip.geojson` derives from [CAL FIRE FRAP's official
historical perimeter layer](https://www.fire.ca.gov/what-we-do/fire-resource-assessment-program/fire-perimeters),
feature GlobalID `e1118f7f-b1a8-43b6-b6d0-d03fe3b1136e`, incident number
`00016737`, final area 153,335.6 acres. It is intersected with the 2025 town
boundary and simplified to 0.00005 degree **for display only**. It is a final
outcome, not an observed time series; inclusion in the polygon does not prove
that every location burned. The default map does not show this historical layer.

`terrain_3dep_7x7.json` holds 49 elevation samples from the [USGS 3DEP
Elevation Point Query Service](https://epqs.nationalmap.gov/v1/docs), generated
by `scripts/build_paradise_terrain.py`. The service reported 1 m underlying
resolution and an acquisition date of August 5, 2022 for every sample, but
the **display mesh is only 7 × 7 samples across the town bounding box**.
Between points, the 3D view interpolates heights and exaggerates vertical
relief. It is not a DEM suitable for modeling local fire spread or roads.

`GET /api/v1/paradise/live` fetches current public NOAA/NWS hourly forecast
data and NIFC WFIGS incident points. It additionally fetches NASA FIRMS VIIRS
detections when `FIRMS_MAP_KEY` is configured. Each response reports fetch time,
source state and errors. A five-minute process cache limits upstream calls.
Zero records from a query do not indicate zero fire risk. Forecast periods are
future meteorological estimates, and satellite detections are neither complete
coverage nor authoritative fire perimeters.

The interactive forecast below the Paradise public-data panel is still the
synthetic exercise. None of these public observations are silently ingested
into that exercise or treated as a validated spread prediction.

## Acceptance gaps for Paradise operations

1. Obtain time-stamped Paradise-area fire progression observations suitable
   for **out-of-sample** evaluation. [NIST's Camp Fire timeline](https://www.nist.gov/programs-projects/wildland-urban-interface-wui-fire-data-collection-parcel-vulnerabilities/nist/fire)
   is an authoritative reconstruction, but its mapped observations require
   a reviewed machine-readable extraction before use.
2. Aggregate documented, current full-resolution USGS 3DEP terrain and
   LANDFIRE fuel layers to actual model cells. Measure resolution and
   missingness; the 49 visualization samples do not pass this gate.
3. Obtain geography-specific population exposure, evacuation zones, road
   status/capacity, shelter status and response-resource feeds. Private
   resident and responder locations require agreements and access controls.
4. Calibrate and independently validate spread and evacuation models; publish
   error, uncertainty, false-alarm and miss rates for held-out events.
5. Test feed freshness, outage behavior, security, accessibility and human
   approval with the responsible Paradise/Butte County agencies before any
   public-safety deployment. The application cannot issue official orders.

## Reproducible FIRMS history build

`scripts/build_paradise_firms_history.py` downloads NASA FIRMS standard-
processing VIIRS detections for November 8–9, 2018 from both S-NPP and NOAA-20.
It removes duplicate detections, validates required fields and coordinates,
and requires at least four unique acquisition times before marking the frozen
package acceptable for historical replay. Configure `FIRMS_MAP_KEY` only in
the environment; the key is never stored in the generated JSON or logs.

The generated `camp_fire_firms_2018.json` remains an observation-point layer.
It must be compared with the independent NIST fire-progression reconstruction
and must not be presented as an authoritative perimeter.

`camp_fire_nist_timeline.json` records four continuous NIST observation-map
windows from 06:25 through 10:00 PST and the verified SHA-256 identity of every
source PDF. This is sufficient for source-backed visual validation. Numerical
model scoring remains blocked until the map observations are digitized and
reviewed with a documented positional-error estimate.

## Historical population exposure build

`scripts/build_paradise_population.py` joins the 2017 Census block-group
geography with 2013–2017 ACS five-year total-population estimates, clips the
result to the official 2018 Paradise place boundary, and records source hashes,
coverage, missing joins and sampling margins of error. The output is an
area-weighted exposure estimate for the Camp Fire replay. It contains no
addresses, live occupancy, household records or individual resident locations.

Configure `CENSUS_API_KEY` only in the environment. The key is never stored in
the generated file. Until the quality report reports full boundary coverage and
no missing population joins, the exposure layer must remain blocked from model
claims.

## Shared grid and data quality report

`paradise_grid_500m.json` is generated by `scripts/build_paradise_grid.py`. It
maps the town-clipped ACS population, 2025 TIGER/Line roads, 3DEP display
samples and the FIRMS query area to one documented 500 m local grid (841 cells,
3 km town buffer, stable `P-row-col` IDs). Each cell records source, method and
an explicit status. Fuel is a declared default; water, critical facilities and
official shelters are marked missing or scenario. The grid is not EPSG:32610 and
must not be described as a survey product.

`data_quality_report.json` is generated by
`scripts/build_paradise_data_quality.py`. It is the machine-readable source of
truth for the website's data-status panel. It currently declares the FIRMS
morning gap and 688-minute overnight gap, the 2013–2017 ACS vintage and 17.6%
population reconciliation gap, the 2025 road vintage, the 7×7 post-fire terrain
mesh, and four blocked items: NIST spatial digitization, fuel, water and
critical facilities.

`nist_source_verification.json` records a re-download and SHA-256 check of all
four NIST map PDFs. The PDFs are not committed. Their symbols are embedded in a
14,370×9,746 raster and remain undigitized, so NIST is used as a temporal
constraint only.

Rebuild locally with `scripts/requirements-build.txt` (shapely, pyshp, httpx,
optional pymupdf). These build dependencies are not installed in the LinuxONE
runtime image.

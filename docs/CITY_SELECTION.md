# City-selection checklist

Select one US city only after confirming that at least one historical wildfire event has:

- time-stamped hotspots or active-fire detections;
- incident/perimeter snapshots over time;
- hourly or finer wind, humidity and temperature observations;
- fuel/vegetation and terrain coverage;
- road network, evacuation zones or closure records;
- population, buildings and critical-facility exposure data;
- sufficient timestamps to recreate what was available at each replay step.

Prefer completeness and a demonstrable response decision over incident size. Replace `config/city.example.json` and add source adapters; do not hard-code the chosen city inside the model.

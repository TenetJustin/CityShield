- [ ] Real grid and point/polygon-to-cell mapper.
- [ ] One complete independently sourced historical fire incident (the replay clock and synthetic contract are complete).
- [ ] LANDFIRE/3DEP aggregation and exposure layers.
- [ ] Authoritative roads, closures, zones and response resources.
- [ ] Event-level validation and calibrated claims.

## External environment evidence

- [ ] LinuxONE allocation credentials and captured `s390x` execution evidence.

## Claims allowed now

CityShield is a working, LinuxONE-ready research prototype that demonstrates streaming ingestion, state update, ensemble What-if forecasting, constrained advisory decisions, human review, auditability and controlled improvement.

## Claims not allowed now

Do not claim a chosen city, live operational data, validated fire-spread accuracy, calibrated probability, optimized evacuation route, official warning capability, automatic dispatch, deployed RSI, or confirmed LinuxONE execution before the corresponding evidence exists.
